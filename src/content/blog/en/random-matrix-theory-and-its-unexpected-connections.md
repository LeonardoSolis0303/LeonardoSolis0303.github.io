The Wigner-Dyson distribution appears in places that have nothing to do with each other: heavy atomic nuclei, financial correlation matrices, zeros of the Riemann zeta function.

This cannot be a coincidence. There must be an underlying structure connecting all these systems.

My working hypothesis: what they share is that they are all systems with many coupled degrees of freedom, where microscopic details do not matter — only the statistical structure. RMT captures that universality.
