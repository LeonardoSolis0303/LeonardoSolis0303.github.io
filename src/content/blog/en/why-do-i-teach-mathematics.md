I founded Sequoia without being very clear about why. Four years later I have a more honest answer.

Learning something difficult with someone who enjoys it changes how you relate to difficulty. It does not make things easier. It makes you more comfortable with them being hard.

That is what I try to teach. Not calculus. Not physics. The posture toward the problem.
