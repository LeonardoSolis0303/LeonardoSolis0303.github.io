The electricity market runs on a network of nodes. Each node has a price. Understanding how those prices are calculated requires thinking of the system as an optimization problem over a huge graph.

The DC model simplifies the physics: it assumes lines are purely reactive, voltages are constant, and angles are small. With those simplifications, the problem becomes linear.

PTDFs (Power Transfer Distribution Factors) are the coefficients of that linearization.
