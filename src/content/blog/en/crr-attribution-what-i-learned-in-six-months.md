I left the job to finish my thesis, but before leaving I wanted to document the quant project I had started. A CRR attribution layer is not glamorous, but it teaches you to think about risk in a very concrete way.

The most important thing I learned: the difference between a model that works and one that is correct. Our model worked. I am not sure it was correct.

Stack: PostgreSQL for historical data, SQLAlchemy as ORM, Pandas for analysis, Azure Data Studio for visualization.
