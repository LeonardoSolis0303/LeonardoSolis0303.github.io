I have been thinking for weeks about why wave function collapse remains so conceptually uncomfortable. It is not that there are no technical answers — there are several. None of them fully satisfies me.

The Copenhagen interpretation says: the wave function collapses upon measurement. What does "measurement" mean? It does not say. Many worlds says: no collapse, everything happens. Why do we see only one outcome? That is not clearly explained either.

My current position: the problem is not technical, it is conceptual. We need a better understanding of what information means in the quantum context before we can give a satisfying answer.
