Not a tutorial. More of an honest reconstruction of what happened: what worked, what almost ruined everything at 2am, and why algebraic geometry turned out to be the key.

We started with an overly ambitious idea. At 2am we had to scrap half the work and rethink. What remained was simpler but more solid.

The key turned out to be a connection between positive Grassmannians and the quantum circuit we were designing. We did not plan it — we found it along the way.
